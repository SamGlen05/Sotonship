We keep one of each in the model, so a ship's reasons do not double-count the same fact. The longest-gap measure tracks how often a ship was seen, so the model uses gaps inside receiver coverage instead.

    Dark vessels: what satellite radar sees

    Sentinel-1 radar images ships whether or not they broadcast AIS. Global Fishing Watch matches each radar detection to AIS; a detection with no AIS match is a vessel that was not broadcasting at that place and time. Detections cover vessels above roughly 15 to 20 m of every type and depend on how often the satellites passed, so trends and shares matter more than counts.
